# Ekoplazm: dub siren and delay-reverb synth

Ekoplazm is a 2-in-1 analogue synth. It has a classic dub siren and a delay and reverb section with feedback. It is number 3 in my "Little synths with BIG sounds" series.

![Ekoplazm](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/af7e4dc1-853e-424f-8df0-0d4b26520de4)

You can play the dub siren through the delay and reverb section, for a rich and full sound. You can also connect other synths to the delay and reverb section, for example my [Proton](https://github.com/lonesoulsurfer/Proton_-_Little_Synths_With_BIG_Sounds_No2) and [Elements](https://github.com/lonesoulsurfer/Elements-Little_Synths_With_BIG_Sounds_-1) synths.

## What you need

Ekoplazm does not use a microcontroller. You do not need software or firmware.

- All parts are in the [parts list](Parts%20List/) (PDF and Excel).
- The schematic is [Ekoplazm Schematic.pdf](Ekoplazm%20Schematic.pdf).
- The Gerber files for the PCB and the front panel are in [Gerber Files](Gerber%20Files/). You can send them to a PCB maker.

## Build it

The Instructables guide above shows each step, with photos. In short:

1. Order the PCB and the front panel with the Gerber files.
2. Get the parts in the parts list.
3. Solder the parts to the PCB.
4. Attach the front panel.
5. Connect the power and test the synth.
